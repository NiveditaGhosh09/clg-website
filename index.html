PLOT 08 · THE INSTITUTE

About the College

Situated in the industrial township of New Town, Kolkata, Techno International New Town (formerly Techno India College of Technology) began its journey in 2005 with a group of renowned academicians and experienced professionals from industry.

The focus of the institution is to accelerate knowledge transfer and enrich teaching-learning, research and social outreach — delivering quality education across B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, MBA, BBA and BCA programs.
